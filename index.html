Why Cognitive AI Agents

HUMANIZE AUTOMATION WITH SOFT SKILLS

Typical Artificial Intelligence (AI) agents or assistants powered by large language models (LLMs) like GPT automate routine tasks, such as answering a question. To aid in high-touch tasks, AI agents must be powered with cognitive intelligence—advanced human soft skills, such as active listening and reading between the lines, in addition to language skills.

Empathetic Automation

Like a proactive human counselor, cognitive AI agents are proactive. They are powered by Interactional Intelligence, such as active listening, to guide users purposefully toward a goal. They scale out high-touch human services (e.g., career counseling and patient followup) and relieve administrative burdens.

Psychographic Insights

Like a human psychologist, cognitive AI agents are perceptive. They are powered by Personal Intelligence, such as reading between the lines to automatically infer user psychographic insights from a conversation. Such insights, including user personality, deepen personalized persuasions (e.g., student retention and patient adherence).

Authentic Connection

With an unassuming and non-judgemental personality, cognitive AI agents engage users in open, two-way conversations and build authentic connections with them. Such interactions provide users with psychological safety, reduce social desirability biases, and foster productive human-AI collaboration.
